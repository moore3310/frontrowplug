Questions

Are these official box-office tickets?+

FrontRowPlug is a resale marketplace. Tickets come from season-ticket holders, brokers and fans who can’t go, so prices are set by the market and can be above or below the original face value. Every ticket is verified before it’s transferred to you.

How do I receive my tickets?+

Almost everything this season is mobile-only. Once you pay, the tickets are transferred to your phone through the venue’s official ticketing app or a secure mobile link — you just scan in at the door. No printing, no will-call lines.

What if the show is postponed or canceled?+

If an event is postponed, your tickets stay valid for the new date. If it’s canceled outright and not rescheduled, you get a full refund — no forms, no runaround.

Can you get seats for a show that isn’t listed here?+

Almost always, yes. The listings above are the headline dates, but we work shows in every state all season long — arena tours, rivalry games, Broadway stops, comedy runs. Text the show and city to 715-612-0292 and we’ll send a quote.

How fast can I get tickets for a show this week?+

Mobile delivery makes last-minute easy — for most events we can have verified e-tickets on your phone the same day, often within the hour, right up until showtime.
